Why BabyDashNow exists
"It was 10 PM. My son wouldn't stop crying. We had no diapers, no formula — and not a single store open in Stamford."

Last year, a few weeks after my son was born, he refused to nurse one night — and we had run out of both diapers and formula at the same time. I drove around Stamford for over an hour after 10 PM looking for anything open. Nothing.

I sat in that car feeling helpless in a way I hadn't expected. And then I thought — if I'm going through this, every parent in this city goes through this.

That night is why BabyDashNow exists. Not as a company — as a neighbor who shows up. I built this so no parent in Stamford, Darien, Norwalk, or Greenwich ever has to make that drive again.
